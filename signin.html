2. What PlateletWatch does

PlateletWatch uses AI to detect and count platelets, red blood cells and white blood cells in microscope images of blood smears, estimates a platelet count per µL, and compares it with WHO dengue severity thresholds. You can also record confirmatory test results (such as NS1 antigen and Dengue IgM/IgG) and notes from a doctor or medical technologist.

3. Not medical advice

  • Results are estimates and may be inaccurate, especially with poor image quality, focus, lighting or staining.
  • Do not start, stop or change any treatment based only on this app.
  • If you or someone else shows dengue warning signs (severe abdominal pain, persistent vomiting, bleeding, drowsiness or restlessness, difficulty breathing) or the app reports a Danger or Critical result, seek medical care immediately.
